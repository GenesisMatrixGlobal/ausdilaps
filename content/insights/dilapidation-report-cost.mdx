A pre-construction dilapidation report on a standard house starts **from $505 + GST**. The matching post-construction report starts **from $610 + GST**. Units start from **$420 each**, and commercial buildings from $505. Road and infrastructure work is quoted per project.

These are starting prices, not fixed rates. Projects with more properties usually come in lower per property, and the final price depends on the scope. Every price on this page excludes GST.

## Houses, townhouses and units

- **Single-storey house or townhouse:** from $505 pre-construction, from $610 post-construction.
- **Two-storey house or townhouse:** from $550 pre-construction, from $745 post-construction.
- **Apartment or unit:** from $420 each.

A single property on its own is usually priced at our $505 minimum call-out. When we inspect several properties on the same project, the per-property price comes down.

## Commercial buildings

- **Up to 500m²:** from $505.
- **500m² to 1,500m²:** from $975.
- **Over 1,500m²:** priced by floor area.

The post-construction report usually costs about 20% more than the pre-construction one.

## Why the post-construction report costs more

The post-construction report is a matching inspection. The inspector works from the pre-construction record and documents the same areas again, so any change after the works is evidenced against the baseline. Matching takes longer than recording, so it costs more.

You don't have to book both at once. But a pre-construction report on its own only shows where things stood. It's the pair that proves whether the works caused any damage.

## Roads, infrastructure and large projects

These are quoted per project. Road condition surveys are priced by area for GPS-located photo surveys, or per lane for video surveys. The quote also depends on traffic management, night work and site inductions.

On a project with dozens of adjoining properties, volume brings the per-property price down. You get an itemised quote, not a lump sum.

## Extras that can change the total

- **Defect-marked floor plans:** from $75 per floor.
- **RPEQ engineer sign-off:** from $195 per report.
- **Access letters:** from $35 per property. We post up to three letters, then follow up by SMS and doorknock.
- **Site inductions:** charged by the hour when a site requires one. Short inductions of 15 minutes or less are free.
- **Night and weekend work:** a loading applies.
- **Travel to remote sites:** charged by the hour, to and from site.
- **Printed copies:** available on request.

## What makes one job cost more than another

- **Number of properties.** More properties means a lower price per property, but a bigger total.
- **Size and type.** A two-storey house takes longer than a unit. A warehouse takes longer than both.
- **Access.** Owners who don't reply hold up the program. We can run the access letters for you.
- **Capture method.** Photo, video, drone and GPS surveys are priced differently. We match the method to the asset.
- **Timing and location.** Night work, weekends and travel all add cost.

## Who pays for a dilapidation report?

Generally the property owner, builder or developer arranges and pays for it. Councils often make it a condition of development approval. If you live next to a development, ask the builder whether a report on your property is already booked before you order your own.

## Is a cheaper report worth it?

A dilapidation report is only worth what it can prove when a damage claim is made. Photos without locations, missed rooms or vague defect notes are what lose disputes.
