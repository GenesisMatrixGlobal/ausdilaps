Your recording goes through **Transcription Buddy**, which turns it into text the report team types up in the house style. Everything you say that isn't a figure has to be stripped out again before typing. The less of that there is, the faster your report gets written.

## What the report needs, and nothing else

For every photo, three things, in this order:

1. **The figure number.** Say it every time — "twelve", not "next photo". Numbers are the thing transcription gets wrong most, and a photo count that drifts by one takes an hour to untangle.
2. **The element.** What the photo is of: "retaining wall", "west wall", "ceiling", "boundary fence".
3. **The location.** Where it is, as one room or area: "east yard", "bedroom 1", "entrance hallway".

So a general view is just:

> "Twelve, retaining wall, east yard."

For a defect, add the defect and say **major** out loud if it is one. Anything you don't call major is typed as minor.

> "Fourteen, hairline cracking, east wall, bedroom 2."
> "Twenty-one, major, cracking in the concrete floor, garage."

Use compass directions, never front, back, left or right. Say the level when it changes: "upper level", "lower level", "basement".

## Leave these out

None of this reaches the report, and all of it has to be removed by hand or by the tool:

- Your name and a greeting ("Hi, this is …")
- "Today I'm going to do the pre-inspection at …" — the address is on the file name and the work order
- Temperature and weather
- "No one responding to my door knock", "as per the work order", "I'm just going to do the external inspection" — put access problems in your site notes, not the recording
- "That's all for today", "thank you"
- Fillers: "so", "now", "just", "which is"
- "Along with" — say "and"
- Commentary like "that is the only photo I could capture"

## Name the file after the job

Save the recording as the address: `11 Emeraldwood St.mp3`. The tool reads the street name off the file name and uses it to hear the address correctly, so a garbled suburb in the audio stops mattering.

## One recording, one property

Start a new recording for each property. The report team types one job at a time and a batch is copied in the order the files were dropped.
