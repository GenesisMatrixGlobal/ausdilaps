I've had a letter from AusDilaps. What is it?

A construction project near you has engaged us to record the condition of nearby properties before work starts. The report is an independent photographic record of your property, so there's a clear baseline if anything changes.

Who is responsible for getting the report?

Usually whoever is carrying out the works (the builder, developer or owner of the site being developed) arranges and pays for it, and councils may require it as an approval condition. If you've had a letter from us, the project near you has engaged us.

How long does the inspection take?

A standard residential inspection typically takes 15 to 45 minutes. Writing the report can then take several days to weeks, depending on complexity.

What happens if the report finds issues?

The report sets out each problem and its severity, with recommendations such as crack repair. It then becomes the reference point for any new damage during the works.

Can I get a copy of my report?

Property owners can ask us for a copy of their inspection report.
